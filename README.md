## Analytical Workflow

I follow a structured workflow to ensure that each project addresses a clear business problem.

- [x] Understand the dataset and its structure
- [x] Explore data quality and identify potential anomalies
- [ ] Clean and validate the data
- [ ] Define business metrics and analytical questions
- [ ] Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- [ ] Build visualizations and interactive dashboards
- [ ] Develop evidence-based business recommendations
- [ ] Document findings and project limitations

*Checklist items describe my general project workflow, not the completion status of every individual project.*

## Learning Approach

I dedicate focused time to each project to improve my technical skills and business understanding. My approach emphasizes more than just producing charts or writing queries.

I aim to understand:

- **Business context:** Why does the problem matter?
- **Data quality:** Is the data reliable enough for analysis?
- **Analytical reasoning:** What patterns can the data support?
- **Business insights:** What do the results mean?
- **Recommendations:** What actions might be worth considering?
- **Documentation:** Can another person understand and reproduce the analysis?
