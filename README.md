## Analytical Workflow

I follow a structured workflow to connect data analysis with marketing and business questions.

- [x] Understand the dataset and its structure
- [x] Explore data quality and identify potential anomalies
- [ ] Clean and validate the data
- [ ] Define business questions and relevant KPIs
- [ ] Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- [ ] Analyze customer behavior and performance trends
- [ ] Build visualizations and interactive dashboards
- [ ] Develop evidence-based business recommendations
- [ ] Document findings and project limitations

*Checklist items describe my general workflow, not the completion status of every individual project.*

## Learning Approach

I aim to go beyond reporting numbers by understanding the business context behind the data.

- **Business context:** What marketing or customer problem needs to be solved?
- **Data quality:** Is the data reliable enough for analysis?
- **Analytical reasoning:** What patterns can the available data support?
- **Marketing insights:** What do the findings mean for customer behavior or performance?
- **Recommendations:** What actions are worth testing or considering?
- **Documentation:** Can another person understand and reproduce the analysis?
